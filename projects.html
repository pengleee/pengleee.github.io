Selected projects

A focused selection connecting NLP methods with questions about language.

Scope ambiguity in large language models

Ongoing work on how LLMs interpret quantifier scope, and how their responses relate to linguistic structure and human interpretation.

Semantic search & retrieval-augmented generation

SentenceTransformers and FAISS as a basis for exploring semantic similarity, document retrieval, and evidence-grounded generation.

Linguistic markers of misinformation

A collaborative study of political misinformation using LIAR, TF-IDF and Random Forest classification, and VADER / DistilBERT sentiment analysis.
