Academic background & research interests

My background is in Data Science and natural language processing. I am moving toward computational linguistics, formal semantics, and psycholinguistics, with a particular interest in linguistic representation in large language models.

This transition is shaped by a desire to connect computational methods with explicit questions about meaning: what an interpretation is, what makes it available, and how we can study the conditions under which it is preferred.
