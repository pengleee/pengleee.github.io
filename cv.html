Research experience

ScopeShift — Preceding Questions Shift Continuation Preferences after Scope-Ambiguous Sentences in Language Models

Jul 2026 – present

Independent research; manuscript in preparation

  • Designed controlled experiments varying preceding questions while keeping scope-ambiguous replies fixed, using 120 confirmation sentence frames and three language models.
  • Observed consistent context-dependent shifts in singular-versus-plural continuation probabilities across the three models.
  • Tested alternative explanations using unambiguous control sentences and discourse configurations; distinguished continuation preferences from direct evidence of scope assignment.
