Research

ScopeShift

Manuscript in preparation · Independent research · July 2026 – present · Paper (PDF)

Does a preceding question shift which construal of a scope-ambiguous sentence a language model expects? A pilot on 8 development items fixed the design; a frozen confirmation study on 120 new items and three models found a shift in the predicted direction in every model, together with clear limits on what the measure can show.

Course projects

  • Linguistic markers of misinformation

    DSAN 5000 · Fall 2024

    Six-way truthfulness classification of LIAR’s short fact-checked political statements with TF-IDF features and a Random Forest, plus part-of-speech and sentiment comparisons. Accuracy was 23%, against 22% for always predicting the most common label.

  • End-to-end retrieval-augmented generation

    DSAN 5800 Advanced NLP · Fall 2024

    Document-grounded question answering with SentenceTransformers embeddings, a FAISS index, cross-encoder reranking, and a Streamlit interface with document upload and source attribution.
